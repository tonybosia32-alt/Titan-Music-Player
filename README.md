# Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the **Titan Music Player** mobile application (the "Application") for mobile devices, which was created by the Service Provider as a **Free** service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

## 1. Information Collection and Use
The Application collects information when you download and use it. This information may include details such as:
* Your device's Internet Protocol (IP) address
* The operating system running on your mobile device
* Application performance data and usage statistics

The Application does not gather precise real-time location information of your mobile device.

## 2. Sensitive Device Permissions
To provide its core audio playback functionality, the Application requests specific access to your device's files. We use these permissions strictly for the following purposes:
* **Storage and Music Files Access:** Necessary to scan, locate, and read the local audio files stored on your device so that they can be populated and played within the Application.
* **Photos/Media Access:** Used exclusively if you choose to load or customize album art, playlist covers, or background themes from your device's media gallery.

**Data Privacy Guarantee:** All access to your files happens entirely on your local device. **Titan Music Player does not upload, transfer, copy, or share your personal local music files, photos, or data to any external servers or third parties.**

## 3. Third-Party Access
The Application utilizes third-party services that have their own independent Privacy Policies to help improve performance, handle updates, or analyze basic usage. Below are the links to the privacy policies of the standard external service providers used by the Application:

* [Google Play Services](https://google.com)

## 4. Data Retention Policy
The Service Provider will retain any automatically collected data for as long as you use the Application and for a reasonable time thereafter. Since the Application is a localized music player and does not require user accounts, no personal identity profiles are created or stored.

## 5. Children's Privacy
The Service Provider does not use the Application to knowingly solicit data from or market to children under the age of 13. If a parent or guardian becomes aware that his or her child has provided information without their consent, they should contact the Service Provider so the information can be removed.

## 6. Security
The Service Provider is concerned about safeguarding the confidentiality of your information and provides physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards to protect any information processed and maintained by the Application.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time for any reason. The Service Provider will notify you of any changes by updating this page with the new Privacy Policy. You are advised to consult this Privacy Policy regularly for any changes, as continued use is deemed approval of all changes.

## 8. Contact Us
If you have any questions regarding privacy while using the Application, or have questions about our practices, please reach out to us via your developer support options on the Google Play Store.
